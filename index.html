⌘ Prompt Engineering — Top 10

1. Chain of Thought

Force the AI to think step-by-step before answering. Dramatically improves accuracy on complex tasks.

"Think step by step. First analyze the problem, then consider possible approaches, then provide your solution with reasoning."

2. Role Assignment

Assign the AI a specific expert persona for domain-specific quality.

"You are a senior software architect with 20 years of experience. Review this code for security vulnerabilities, performance issues, and architectural concerns."

3. Few-Shot Examples

Provide 2-3 examples of the exact input/output format you want before your actual request.

"Here are examples of the format I need: [Example 1: Input → Output] [Example 2: Input → Output]. Now do the same for: [Your actual request]"

4. Constraint Setting

Set clear boundaries — word count, format, tone, audience, and what NOT to include.

"Write a 200-word summary for a non-technical audience. Use simple language, no jargon. Format as bullet points. Do NOT include code examples."

5. Iterative Refinement

Start broad, then narrow down. Ask the AI to critique and improve its own output.

"Now review your answer above. Identify 3 weaknesses or areas for improvement. Then rewrite a stronger version addressing those issues."

6. Structured Output

Request specific output formats — JSON, markdown tables, numbered lists, or custom templates.

"Respond in this exact JSON format: { \"title\": \"...\", \"summary\": \"...\", \"key_points\": [...], \"action_items\": [...], \"confidence\": 0-100 }"

7. Negative Prompting

Tell the AI what to avoid. Reducing unwanted patterns improves relevance.

"Do NOT use clichés, filler phrases, or generic advice. Avoid phrases like 'in today's fast-paced world' or 'it's important to note that'. Be direct and specific."

8. Context Loading

Front-load relevant context before your question. More context = better answers.

"Context: I'm a [role] working on [project]. My audience is [description]. The goal is [objective]. The constraints are [limitations]. Given this context: [your question]"

9. Multi-Perspective Analysis

Ask the AI to analyze from multiple viewpoints to get more balanced answers.

"Analyze this decision from 3 perspectives: (1) a risk-averse CFO, (2) an innovation-focused CTO, and (3) the end-user. For each, list pros, cons, and recommendation."

10. Metacognitive Prompting

Ask the AI to rate its own confidence and identify what it doesn't know.

"Answer this question, then rate your confidence (1-10) and explain what information you're missing or uncertain about. Flag any assumptions you've made."
